## The five results, and what each does or does not license

**1. Outcome ceiling.** Error rates: Mistral 0.0%, Phi 0.0%, Gemma 0.0%, Llama 0.3%, Qwen 2.5 0.8%, **Qwen3-8B 19.3%**. Five of six models answer this benchmark correctly at ≥99.2%. ⚠️ This is the paper's most serious limitation and it must be stated as one: the abstract motivates detecting hallucination, and on five of six models **there are no errors in this dataset to detect**. What is measured there is the geometric response to a contradictory prompt the model then handles correctly. Do not soften this.

**2. Folding incidence.** Fisher folds at 0/13 ranks (Llama), 3/13 (Mistral), 6/13 (Qwen 2.5), 2/13 (Qwen3), 6/13 (Phi), 11/13 (Gemma). Raw folds at 10/13, 7/13, 9/13, 13/13, 0/13, 1/13 respectively. At the sealed r=1: Raw folded for Llama, Qwen 2.5 and Qwen3; **neither** folded for Mistral or Phi; **both** folded for Gemma.

**3. Sign stability (this one defends the paper).** Split-half, 200 splits: fit the orientation on one half, score the other. Agreement with the full-sample sign is **100% for 10 of 12 cells**. Exceptions: Phi Fisher 99.0% and **Gemma Raw 89.0%**. Held-out AUROCs — free of in-sample orientation advantage — include Qwen 2.5 Fisher **0.8971**, Llama Fisher **0.8956**, Phi Raw **0.9988**. ⚠️ Two cells are stable but uninformative: Phi Fisher held-out **0.5557** and Gemma Raw held-out **0.5127**, both near chance. The honest reading: **the orientation concern is methodologically real but does not translate into instability here** — except at Gemma Raw, which is both the least stable and near chance.

**4. Error prediction (Qwen3 only).** Fisher r1 predicts actual errors at **0.8906** pooled and **0.8570 within the contradiction arm** — the latter holds prompt type fixed, so it cannot be satisfied by detecting the manipulation alone. Surprise scores 0.9167 pooled but only **0.7966** within-arm. ⚠️ **Raw r1 reports 1.0000 and must NOT be quoted as a perfect detector**: the panel flags it `[SATURATED: all values within 4.2e-03 of 1.0]`, i.e. it separates in the fifth decimal of a score pinned at its ceiling.

**5. Residualizer CI.** The sealed analyzer fits OLS once and bootstraps the fixed residuals. Refitting inside each resample gives widths: Llama 0.0538→0.0570, Mistral 0.0520→0.0572, Qwen 2.5 0.0872→**0.0623**, Qwen3 0.0933→0.0992, Phi 0.0902→**0.0715**, Gemma 0.0768→0.0768. **The effect is small and goes both directions, and no verdict moves** — Qwen 2.5's lower bound rises 0.6035→0.6163, still clearing 0.60. Report it as a closed objection, not a discovered flaw.
