**1. Is holding PRI right?**

A short editorial hold is right. An indefinite scientific hold is wrong for the publication I recommend.

PRI contains two distinct kinds of knowledge. First, specific geometric statistics discriminate the synthetic contradiction condition, with a surviving amended-protocol E18 result and a substantial Qwen head-to-head. Second, the investigation identifies concrete ways an apparently successful detector evaluation can answer the wrong question. These contributions survive the loss of hallucination detection as the application claim. The benchmark's ceiling makes it unsuitable for establishing error prediction; it does not make the measured prompt-label associations nonexistent.

The strongest case for publication is the combination, not the author's willingness to confess mistakes. A reader can learn that rank choice reverses an acceptance decision, that coordinate alignment changes a method comparison, that pooled AUROC can conceal subgroup behavior even without sign folding, and that a label called “incorrect” can actually encode response formatting. The surviving Mistral rank-1 site is particularly useful: all six relevant directional AUROCs exceed chance, so orientation cannot explain away the pooled-versus-stratum reversal. That is a concrete counterexample to trusting a pooled method ranking without examining its components. [PRI methods and results](/Users/msrk/Documents/the_GOAT/wiki/paper/pri-draft.tex:337)

The Qwen result also deserves to remain visible. The reported strength comparison is +0.157, and descriptive split-half checks support stable sign calibration within this dataset. But +0.636 is **a different comparison**, obtained by forcing both methods to point in the same hypothesized direction. It is not an improved estimate of superiority over a sign-calibrated static baseline. A negative association can be a useful discriminator after calibration. The same fairness applies to surprise and PRI v1: their inverted signs do not, by themselves, disqualify their discrimination-strength results. The memo should not inherit the steward's habit of accepting folding for PRI's headline and rejecting it when a baseline benefits.

Nor should the paper sell self-correction as proof that its original scientific guarantees held. The amendment followed a rank sweep; 50 rows are shared between the 200-row preliminary and 600-row powered runs—25% of the preliminary sample, 8.3% of the powered sample. The log also records that the first repository snapshot of the plan postdates the experiments. That does not establish misconduct or prove the chronology false, but it prevents an independently timestamped preregistration claim on the evidence inspected. Report the chronology as documented, distinguish it from externally verifiable timing, and call the results evaluations of the amended protocol. [September 10 meta-audit](/Users/msrk/Documents/the_GOAT/wiki/log.md:4615)

The checker is a useful artifact, not a scientific guarantor. It caught a real rank-attribution error; it cannot determine what a label measures or whether prose contradicts a caption. The log records unresolved numerical coverage and “zero broken anchors,” which are different from a clean check of every claim. Likewise, the reported 276/276 reproduction concerns archived analysis fields, not fresh generation or the entire manuscript. These limitations belong in its documentation. [Checker record](/Users/msrk/Documents/the_GOAT/wiki/log.md:4391)

There is also a concrete reason not to release the present PDF: PRI's introduction still calls Gemma's flip an orientation artifact, while the corrected results explicitly reject that explanation. Its abstract still opens with pre-commitment hallucination detection. These are unfinished corrections, not requests for another experiment. **3. Honest framing for the portfolio**

Use this program-level description:

> Furnace Research studies how lightweight measurements of attention, residual motion, and readout geometry distinguish logical contradiction, candidate-answer correctness, and source faithfulness in supplied inputs near the start of a model's response. The studies examine calibration, transfer, redundancy, and sensitivity to measurement choices. They do not establish detection of errors in the measured model's own free-generated answers.

That is a legitimate representation-measurement program. Its useful question is which internal summaries expose task-label information, under what calibration, and with what dependence on model and protocol. It need not be a safety product to be interesting. For example, CC distinguishes a fitting procedure that often succeeds locally from a frozen sign that fails to transfer; RPV asks whether a new statistic adds anything after a stronger baseline is included. Those are substantive questions that a high AUROC alone cannot answer.

Keep three targets separate in every paper: **the input's gold label; the answer the model selects; whether that answer is correct**. A feature associated with the first has not thereby predicted either of the latter two. CC's canonical-token audit establishes that the elicitation protocol produced an admissible response form; it does not turn candidate-faithfulness labels into model-error labels. Its shuffled-label control supports association beyond a shuffled null, not semantic understanding or immunity to dataset shortcuts. The specific control reported in today's comparison uses three permutations; describe that accurately rather than implying an exhaustive null study. [Portfolio comparison](/Users/msrk/Documents/the_GOAT/wiki/log.md:4775)

“Commit moment” should become an operational term with a precise location, not a psychological interpretation. ACE reads the last prompt position before generation. PRI needs the first selected token fed back before its second state exists. Formatting tokens and answer-bearing tokens are not interchangeable. Use “early-response geometry” as the common umbrella, and state each study's actual capture position. A one-token buffer is a possible implementation for PRI; neither a useful hallucination guard nor its runtime overhead was established here.

Replace CC's title with **“Calibrating Early-Response Geometry for Input-Label Discrimination Across Language Models.”** Keep the registered six-task extension in the subtitle. Put the supplied-candidate construct in the first abstract paragraph, where readers currently encounter a monitoring promise. Replace “deployable” in reader-facing interpretation with “clears the registered discrimination criterion,” while preserving the historical endpoint name and definition. An AUROC lower bound above chance does not establish acceptable precision, false-alarm cost, or practical utility.

Two further wording disciplines preserve the contribution. Say “checkpoint-dependent” or “model-dependent,” since six selected checkpoints do not isolate architecture from training, tokenizer, size, and quantization. Say “signal beyond the tested magnitude/confidence controls,” rather than statistical independence in general. Preserve every registered failure and its original bar; changing the application framing is no license to change the result.

**4. Reframed PRI title and abstract**

**Title: Early-Response Geometry Discriminates Synthetic Contradictions: A Comparison of Probability-Weighted and Static Subspaces**

**Abstract:** Lightweight geometric measurements of language-model states can distinguish properties of supplied prompts, but the interpretation of those measurements depends on score orientation, subspace rank, and capture position. We compare probability-weighted and static unembedding subspaces for discriminating synthetic logical contradictions across six 4-bit-quantized open-weight models, using 600 prompts per model. The score measures the fraction of the post-normalization hidden-state difference between the last prompt position and first generated-token position lying outside a chosen subspace. The weighted construction uses an uncentered, support-truncated Fisher approximation; the static construction is a HARP-style control. Under the amended rank-1 protocol, the magnitude-residualized endpoint passes on 3/3 primary models against a 2/3 bar, requiring AUROC at least 0.60 and a 95% interval excluding 0.50. On the designated Qwen 2.5 model, the weighted score exceeds the static score by 0.157 AUROC [0.125, 0.190], clearing the 0.02 bar, with each score oriented using evaluation labels. Descriptive split-half checks support sign transfer within this dataset. At rank 1, three models favor each construction; a Mistral pooled-versus-stratum reversal survives unchanged without orientation fitting. The rank was omitted from the original specification and chosen after a development sweep; the larger evaluation partially overlaps preliminary data, and the reported amendment chronology lacks independent timestamp verification. Outcome inspection also limits interpretation: five models answer at least 99.2% correctly, while the sixth's apparent failures predominantly reflect truncated reasoning preambles. These data do not support an error-prediction evaluation. The score additionally requires processing an already selected token, so it is not a pre-selection predictor. We report a bounded comparison of input-label discrimination and document how coordinate alignment, rank selection, aggregation, and outcome labeling change its evidential meaning.

This abstract deliberately does not advertise a universally superior Fisher method, a pristine preregistration, or successful hallucination detection. It also avoids the literal claim that no error rate is measurable: the manuscript records two and five errors on two models. The precise limitation is insufficient valid outcome variation for a credible error-prediction evaluation, with Qwen3's apparent large error population invalidated by formatting. The checker belongs in an artifact-availability sentence once the released package's actual coverage is documented; it need not compete with the empirical result in the abstract.

**6. The strongest argument for publishing none of it yet**

The strongest objection is that the entire portfolio may be a family of elaborate classifiers for benchmark construction choices, with an application narrative attached afterward. A contradiction insertion, distractor choice, candidate answer, or emitted answer token can expose the label without revealing model error, uncertainty, belief, or a mechanism. The studies largely share instruments, models, tasks, and research lineage, so agreement among them offers less independent corroboration than four papers suggest. PRI's rank was development-selected, its powered sample overlaps earlier data, its chronology is not independently secured, and some public rederivation inputs remain absent from the cited repository pins. ACE's winner identities are unstable. RPV's small increment does not establish equivalence, and CC's more mature inference cannot repair a target that differs from the advertised application.

The objection becomes stronger when the release process itself is considered. Today's corrections repeatedly left opposite claims elsewhere in the manuscript. The checker verified numbers while a formatting label was being described as correctness. A same-day withdrawal demonstrates responsiveness, but also that the release boundary failed. “We audited ourselves hard” cannot substitute for a coherent scientific proposition that survives outside the project's own vocabulary. [Withdrawal record](/Users/msrk/Documents/the_GOAT/wiki/log.md:4704)

**I find this persuasive against releasing any of the four current manuscripts unchanged, and against launching them as a hallucination-detection portfolio. I do not find it persuasive against publishing the bounded empirical and methodological record.** These data do not show that geometric error detection fails; they show that this evidence does not establish it. That distinction is itself worth making public when attached to concrete measurements and usable artifacts.

PRI's finite release conditions should be: one consistent account across abstract, body, captions, and conclusion; explicit separation of original specifications, amendments, and retrospective diagnostics; fair treatment of baseline orientation; and a versioned package that distinguishes archived outputs from the inputs needed to rederive them. The draft currently acknowledges that its cited repository pins do not include the required row-level parquets. Either distribute the banked inputs with the release or state the precise reproducibility gap prominently. Document the checker's checked, unchecked, and unresolved claims; never translate “zero broken anchors” into “all numbers verified.” These are repairs to communication and evidence access, not a demand for a favorable new experiment. [PRI reproducibility statement](/Users/msrk/Documents/the_GOAT/wiki/paper/pri-draft.tex:1639)

A successor hallucination study would need actual generated-answer correctness, enough valid errors, a measurement plane demonstrably preceding the target answer, and evaluation separating prompt-label discrimination from correctness prediction. Merely waiting until a later token will not do: the existing log already shows answer leakage. Retrospective alignment to an answer position can support an offline analysis, but an online monitor would additionally need a prospective rule for locating that position. None of this should be promised as a small repair to PRI.

The author should now commission the bounded reframing and release packaging, beginning with CC and then PRI. Success means publishing a clear account of what these measurements establish, including their failed interpretations—not obtaining a positive hallucination result before allowing the existing work to be seen.
