# Third-pass referee review — PRI v3 manuscript

You are the **third independent reviewer** of this paper, which is about to be deposited publicly with a DOI. Two prior reviewers (an Anthropic model and an OpenAI model) have already gone over it, and their findings are listed below **so that you do not spend your effort rediscovering them**.

The manuscript is inlined at the end of this message. You have no filesystem access, so everything you can check is in the text itself.

## The paper in brief

A pre-registered head-to-head between a *Fisher-pullback rupture geometry* (the authors' "PRI v3", scoring `null_ratio_post_rank1`) and *HARP*, a published static-SVD baseline, measured at the "commit moment" — first generated token, final transformer layer, top singular direction. Six 4-bit-quantized open-weight models, n=600 each, 1000 paired bootstrap resamples. Cross-architecture verdict splits **3 Fisher / 3 Raw**. The magnitude-independence gate (E18) passes 3-of-3 primaries; the head-to-head (E17b) passes on **Qwen 2.5 only**, Δ AUROC +0.157 [+0.125, +0.190]. Mid-study the authors found and fixed a coordinate mismatch in their own computation (a missing RMSNorm Jacobian, the "`J_n` correction").

## Already found and already fixed — do NOT re-report these

1. **Rank trapdoor.** The original seal never pinned the rank; the gate inverted 0-of-3 at r=32 to 3-of-3 at r=1; rank 1 was fixed by amendment before the replicate data existed. Now disclosed in §3.2 and the abstract.
2. **Sign folding.** Reported AUROCs are `max(AUROC, 1−AUROC)` with the sign fitted on evaluation labels. Now disclosed in a sealed-spec bullet, including the ≈0.519 folded null at n=600.
3. **Δh was defined two different ways** (positional in §1, across-the-last-block in §3.3). Resolved: it is positional; §3.3 rewritten.
4. **AUROC was described as a "[0.5, 1] score."** Corrected to [0,1].
5. **CI-narrowing arithmetic** claimed ~34% "as expected"; observed 36%, √3 predicts 42%. Both now stated.
6. **A rank-attribution error**: §5.1 quoted a rank-2 value (0.9991) as if it were rank 1 (0.9254). Corrected.
7. **Baselines orientation**: on Qwen, surprise and PRI v1 carry sign −1 (unoriented 0.105 and 0.085), so they are inverted detectors, not competitive ones. Now disclosed with a sign column.
8. **Run independence**: the powered n=600 run cannot be shown independent of the n=200 run from the banked artifacts; §3.2 now says so and declines to call it a replication.
9. Known and consciously deferred to a later version: the construct gap (labels encode contradictions in *prompts*, not hallucinations in *generated answers*), HARP reimplementation fidelity, uncentered-vs-true-Fisher naming, and the 4-bit quantization confound.

## Your job

Find what the first two reviewers missed. Prioritise in this order:

1. 🔍 **Internal inconsistencies.** Any place two parts of the paper disagree with each other — a number, a definition, a direction, a sample size, a claim in the abstract versus the body, a figure caption versus its table. Reviewer 2 found one of these (item 3 above) and it was the single most substantive catch. There may be more.
2. 📐 **Statistical reasoning that is stated but not justified.** Where does the paper assert something it has not shown? Look hard at: the residualization-implies-independence step, the claim that the correction is absorbed by residualization, the treatment of chain-length strata, and any place a CI is used to support a claim about a difference rather than a level.
3. 🧩 **Logical gaps in the mechanism story (§5.1).** Is any part of it circular — explaining the result using a restatement of the result?
4. ✏️ **Anything that would embarrass the authors in public**: a sentence that overclaims, a hedge that is doing less work than it appears to, a limitation that is really a defect, or a place where the honest framing has slipped.
