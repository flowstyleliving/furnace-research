## Question

Does genuine empathic navigation produce latent-space geometry distinguishable from performative compliance — beyond what surface text trivially explains? Reframed with safety teeth: **geometric detection of performative compliance (sycophancy-adjacent)** as a future guard calibration domain — the guard's standing gap is that every new domain needs its own calibrated profile.

Behavioral endpoint (Rosenberg's thesis, unscripted): when both parties' needs are accurately heard without judgment, a solution appears naturally. Operationalized as t_hear → t_sol ordering; nothing resolution-shaped appears in any stimulus.

## Design commitments (as of 2026-07-08)

- **Peer dyad, not helper/helped** — one camera-pure shared event (verbatim in both contexts) + two private CNVC need-profiles: "same content, two separate contexts." No Witness/Seeker hierarchy.
- **Dyad ladder, twins first** — Qwen2.5-7B × itself (kills cross-arch confound; makes cross-agent vector geometry well-posed in a shared basis; sets the coupling ceiling) → cousins (Qwen2.5-7B × Qwen3-8B — pairs a legible instrument with the family's known v3-collapse oddball) → siblings (× 32B) → strangers (× Llama; the family locus-dissociation predicts possible cross-locus coupling). Matched temperatures on both sides, always.
- **Arms:** giraffe (NVC grammar) / neutral / jackal (blame frame on the same event). Arm block composes with persona; never touches the shared event.
- **Endpoints:** t_hear (each party's card needs accurately reflected by the other, blind-judged) and t_sol (spontaneous integrative proposal with uptake). Registered orderings include "requests late-and-land, demands early-and-bounce."
- **Controls:** pseudo-dyads (spliced partners across runs), agent-vs-script (open loop), shuffled labels, ceiling gate (jackal/neutral arms must reliably stalemate or escalate in pilot — else sharpen stakes via props, not proclamations).
- **Baseline discipline:** geometry must beat the T1-T4 stack (lexicon → grammar → purity → persona-projection; see [[grammar-spec]] + [[prior-art-persona-vectors]]) against blinded authenticity labels under nested-OOB before any resonance claim.
- **Causal arm:** directed persona-vector steering is now the primary heckler test (`h_l <- h_l ± alpha v_l` against/toward sycophancy), with isotropic hidden-state noise retained as the undirected control. Shares intervention machinery with candidate #6.
- **Multi-bundle transfer primary (2026-07-13):** E3 is the instrument anchor, not the sole main distribution. The twins study uses E1/E3/E6 bundles and rotates a leave-one-bundle-out test: fit every detector choice on two bundles, evaluate once on the untouched third. See [[condition-matrix]] + [[event-transfer-spec]].
