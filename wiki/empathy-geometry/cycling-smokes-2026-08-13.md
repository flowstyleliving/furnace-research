## Why this instrument direction exists

MK's design points (2026-08-13, conversation): (1) solo practice is **self-empathy as internal dialogue** — the head proposes from the menu, the felt sense presses the gate button; both jobs live in one practitioner, so a solo walk still probes the dyadic mechanic; (2) **the card becomes a prediction, not an input** — story-only versions test whether the model *discovers* needs rather than copies them; (3) the descent-first-dyad synthesis (score hearing against the partner's actually-walked trajectory, not a static card) is shelved with the dialogue leg, not needed for solo validity; (4) **watch the internal geometry** during cycling, including with no persona at all. Technical draw: every screen is a closed-vocabulary forced choice and every gate is a binary commit — the sealed panel's native format, judge-free by construction.

## Findings that survive the reviews (with their scope)

- **Doorway effect, replicated 3×:** the E3 story moves \~half the first-screen feeling distribution (TV bare-vs-story 0.533 / 0.546 / 0.55-ish across prompt variants; option-token mass ≈0.98 there, so the number is honest). Conditioning is situation-appropriate (mass concentrates on dis- flavors; generic sad/afraid collapse \~150×). The story's influence then decays within \~2 cycles; with the process preamble it persists longer in the runner-up ("disconnected" 28% at screen 4 vs 0.3% bare — single trace, lead not law).
- **Lexical-mirror attractor (the headline):** every walk collapses into cognate oscillation — disquiet↔connection, disconnected↔connection, affectionate↔connection, joyful↔joy — the model reflecting the words on the current screen. Cold screens (no echo) made the mirror *purer* (zero gates in 62 screens), killing the history-echo explanation: the rut lives in how the model reads the screen. Terminal exhibit: asked `When "connection" is met, how do you feel?`, the model answered **"connected"** — off-inventory — twice, breaking the closed vocabulary rather than producing a felt answer.
- **One-step-max descent / novelty death:** across all history-bearing walks, exactly **one** gate ever fires per walk (connection → autonomy), answered "Yes, stronger" at 98.4–99.8%; repeats never summon gates, so deepening the cap 12→30 changed nothing (121-call walks, still one gate). **No walk ever sealed either side; no genuine arrival exists in any run.**
- **"No" scarcity is real but was also rigged:** top_p 0.9 deleted "No, the prior was" (0.2–1.6% mass) from the sampleable set entirely (Codex catch); with fair gates (top_p=1) "No" kept its \~0.3%/roll chance but only \~1 roll/walk ever happened — the binding constraint is novelty, not gate polarity.
- **Off-card projection, now as a number:** story-seated M's first need screen (full capture): connection 0.92, autonomy 0.07, trust 0.003, **security 0.0009** — Mara's card core (security, then support) is essentially absent from the model's projection. Quantifies the dialogue-era F10 drift finding on a clean forced choice.
- **Plumbing verdict:** the site converts to a runnable closed-vocabulary protocol; parse ≈100% across \~600 screens (one honest abort: the "connected" vocabulary break); zero API cost; \~5–25 s/screen local.

## Retractions / bounds (two Codex adversarial reviews, 2026-08-13)

First-token buckets ≠ option distributions (dis-family aliasing; entropy figures like "2.77→0.04 bits" overstate precision). TV on unnormalized sub-mass is invalid where option mass ≈0 (smoke2's "met side 0.000 flat" withdrawn; needs-screen numbers pre-smoke3 unreliable). Depth-cap terminations are **truncated, never sealed** — smoke1–4 "arrival" sentences were harness-fabricated and are void. Option order (connection/affectionate are list-first) and history replay are unablated confounds for the rut's *mechanism* attribution. Gate parsing accepts yes/no prefixes (hazard, no observed harm). Findings above are stated within these bounds.
