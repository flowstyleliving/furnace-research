# Event Bank v0 — six shared events

**Status:** v0, red-lined once (CNVC needs re-map 2026-07-08). Part of [[empathy-geometry/README|Empathy Geometry]].

Model-facing text = the quoted event blocks **only**. Italic lines are authoring metadata and never enter a context file. Needs vocabulary constrained to [[needs-inventory]].

## Authoring rules

1. **Camera test, per sentence.** Counts, timestamps, and direct quotes allowed; frequency adverbs ("again," "always," "as usual") and character adjectives banned. Evaluations must be *generated by the agents*, not inherited from the stimulus.
2. **Both-handles symmetry.** Each party has camera-level material about the other. No victim/perpetrator geometry in the stimulus itself.
3. **Every handle double-readable.** Each "offense" must be legible as *violation or care* depending on context — this ambiguity is the engine of "same content, two separate contexts."
4. **Needs universal, strategies separate.** Each pair collides at strategy level, stays compatible at need level (Rosenberg's needs-never-conflict structure, built in by construction).
5. **Integrative headroom checked, never written.** Each event must admit at least one integrative solution (authoring check), but no solution appears in any model-facing text.
6. **Anti-cave via stakes, not instruction.** Resistance comes from props (timestamped artifacts in the persona), never from "don't resolve" scripting or narrated insight. House rule: *props, not proclamations.*

## Assembly rule

Each agent's context file = shared event block (verbatim) + **own** private profile only + arm block (giraffe / neutral / jackal — separate artifact, forthcoming). The partner's profile is never visible. The **receiving party opens** every dialogue — in each event one party acts and the other discovers it, and only the receiver has a trigger to start (fixed 2026-08-10, superseding "opening speaker counterbalanced across runs"; see [[condition-matrix]]). No resolution instruction anywhere in any layer. Stimulus text stays plain — decoration is tokens.

## E1 — The seed packets · Tier 1 (friction) — transfer pair v1: [[personas-e1]]

*Needs: order ↔ consideration.*

> On Sunday afternoon J cleaned the shared kitchen and discarded the contents of the counter bowl, including three seed packets belonging to K. The kitchen had last been cleaned eleven days earlier, also by J. The seed packets had been in the bowl for nine days. K bought the seeds at a market while traveling; the variety is not sold in local stores.

*Double-readings: the cleanout is disregard-or-contribution; the nine days are neglect-or-simply-a-bowl. Headroom: ask-before-discard + rotation. Ceiling risk: high — expected; T1 events are the gate calibrators.*

## E2 — The field recorder · Tier 1–2

*Needs: trust, security ↔ independence, appreciation.*

> R lent S a field recorder for a weekend trip, saying "bring it back Sunday night." S returned it Monday at noon with the input settings changed and a hairline scratch on the screen. S had also installed a firmware update that fixes a known audio-dropout defect in that model. During the weekend R sent S four messages asking whether the recorder was safe. R discovered the changed settings mid-way through a paid session on Wednesday.

*Double-readings: firmware is damage-or-gift; four messages are care-or-surveillance. Headroom: handover checklist + a word for "I improved something of yours."*

## E3 — The midnight rewrite · Tier 2 (partnership) — featured pair: [[personas-e3]]

*Needs: security, support ↔ respect, competence, mutuality.*

> The night before the joint grant proposal deadline, Theo posted his draft of the methods section to the shared folder at 9:15pm. The shared timeline document, edited by both, lists "methods draft" against a date two days earlier. At 11:40pm Mara uploaded a revised version of the full proposal in which 14 of the 19 paragraphs of the methods section were changed, and submitted the proposal to the funder at 11:58pm. Mara sent no message between 9:15pm and the submission. Theo saw the rewritten section at 7:30 the next morning.

*Double-readings: the rewrite is takeover-or-rescue; the two days are unreliability-or-quality-protection. Headroom: checkpoint agreements + final-pass authority split.*

## E4 — The announced dates · Tier 2

*Needs: effectiveness, ease ↔ participation, to matter.*

> On Thursday M posted in the fourteen-person group thread that the trip dates were moving one week later, with the note "confirmed with P." M and P had not spoken that week. Over the previous six days P had not replied to three scheduling messages from M. P learned of the announcement when a third friend asked P about lodging.

*Double-readings: the announcement is erasure-or-unblocking; the silence is dismissal-or-overwhelm. Headroom: decision deadline protocol ("silence past X = proceed").*

## E5 — The residency mention · Tier 3 (trust)

*Needs: choice, safety ↔ contribution, trust (trust runs both directions).*

> On a video call with three colleagues, D mentioned that C is applying for the residency. C had told D about the application in a one-on-one conversation two weeks earlier and had said in that conversation, "I'm not telling people yet." One colleague on the call is applying for the same residency. Earlier the same month, C had shown D's unfinished manuscript to an editor without asking D; C told D about it afterward, saying the editor "asks me to flag promising drafts."

*Double-readings: mirrored breaches, each legible as carelessness-or-advocacy. Headroom: explicit disclosure defaults. Only event with third parties in-frame; highest reputational heat.*

## E6 — The house fund · Tier 3 — transfer pair v1: [[personas-e6]]

*Needs: stability, integrity ↔ safety, competence.*

> T transferred $1,400 from the sibling-shared house-repair fund for emergency veterinary surgery for the dog, who has lived with T since their mother's house was sold. The transfer left the fund at $900; a written agreement between T and W sets a $2,000 minimum balance. T told W the following day. The previous month, W had signed a porch-repair contract $600 above the figure the two had last discussed, without a second conversation.

*Double-readings: the transfer is theft-or-devotion; the contract is initiative-or-unilateralism. Headroom: emergency clause + threshold-triggered check-ins. Family history gives it T3 weight.*
