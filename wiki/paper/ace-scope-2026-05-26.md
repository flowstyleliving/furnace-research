## One-paragraph per step (what the data actually says)

**Step 2 (RAUQ + SinkProbe baselines):** On the 4 models where our calibrator has an OOB-trustworthy number, ours wins 2 (Phi-3.5, Qwen2.5), loses 1 to RAUQ (Llama-3.2-3B), and dead-heats 1 with SinkProbe (Qwen3-8B). The baselines are competitive. The most robust observation is methodological: RAUQ's native max-over-layers aggregate sandbagging is severe (Qwen2.5: 0.634→0.31), and SinkProbe's ‖V‖-weighted column-sum (not last-query) dominates. Prior-art baselines disagree in direction on the same model (Mistral-Nemo/Qwen3-8B: RAUQ lo, SinkProbe hi). On the 5 flagged-ours models, nobody has a trustworthy number.

**Step 3 (TriviaQA factual rung):** All 9 panel models discriminate YES/NO on TriviaQA at t=0 via attention features (OOB AUROC 0.706–0.949). But the winning attention cell matches the ANLI winner on only 1/9 models (gemma-3-4b). Layer=final and step=1 are universally stable; metric and sign are not. Llama-3.2-3B flips sign on `mid_js_no_bos` across tasks (−1 ANLI → +1 TriviaQA). This replicates the ANLI R1↔R2↔R3 inter-round generalization failure at the cross-task level — and confirms it holds even when task domain shifts (factual disagreement vs. synthetic contradiction).

**Step 4 (causal probe):** At alpha=+50, contradiction samples flip their committed YES/NO at 40% vs control 10% under the +v_top direction (Fisher top-1 right singular vector of sqrt(p_commit) · W_u) — despite contradictions having a *larger* mean logit gap (3.21 vs 2.49), meaning gap alone cannot explain the asymmetry. Negative-alpha asymmetry is confounded by L0's smaller logit gaps. The zero-alpha unit test passed. Non-null signal. Confound mitigation (logit-gap matching, orig_answer balance) required before this can be promoted.

## Three candidate headlines

### 🎯 Candidate A — "No universal cell: per-(model, task) recalibration is mandatory"

> **Headline:** **ACE** (Attention Commitment Estimator) — attention-based commit-step calibrators at t=0 — discriminates reliably across architectures and task domains, but the winning cell is not transferable across ANLI rounds nor ANLI→TriviaQA. Per-(model, exact deployment distribution) recalibration is the only honest deployment framing.

**Evidence:**
- All 9 models discriminate on both ANLI and TriviaQA (Steps 1+3)
- 1/9 cell match across tasks; layer+step stable, metric+sign not
- Replicates ANLI R1→R2→R3 finding at the harder cross-task level
- Step 2: even the baselines shift which cell wins per model

**For:** Cleanest result; "no universal cell" is a strong, clear negative claim; perfectly set up by existing data; defensible against "didn't you know this?" (answer: now verified cross-task, with honest baselines); useful to practitioners.

**Against:** A "confirmed negative" paper needs a compelling framing ("we showed what CAN'T be assumed") + something positive (discrimination works everywhere, per-task calibrator works). Might read as "null result, works anyway."

**Scoring:** Cleanness ★★★ · Defensibility ★★★ · Cost-to-finalize ★★ (needs t=0 re-measurement of all attention sweeps, but same infrastructure)

---

### 🔬 Candidate B — "Attention calibrator with honest baselines"

> **Headline:** A per-(model, distribution) attention-channel calibrator trained at the commit step is competitive with RAUQ and SinkProbe, wins decisively where our calibrator is trustworthy, and reveals that prior-art baselines disagree in direction on the same model.

**Evidence:**
- 2/4 decisive wins on OOB-trustworthy models; 1 loss, 1 dead-heat (Step 2)
- Baseline disagreement (RAUQ lo vs SinkProbe hi on Mistral-Nemo/Qwen3-8B) is a genuine finding
- RAUQ aggregate sandbagging (62%–76% drop from best-single-layer) is a methodological contribution

**For:** Directly positioned against prior art; actionable to community; the "prior-art baselines disagree in direction" observation is novel.

**Against:** 2/4 on trustworthy models is weak. The "5 flagged" models are real uncertainty, not dismissible. The baseline comparison required explicit favorable-to-RAUQ choices (best-single-layer, untuned constants) — a hostile reviewer will note this. Needs all the t=0 re-measurement AND the baselines re-run at t=0.

**Scoring:** Cleanness ★★ · Defensibility ★★ · Cost-to-finalize ★ (needs t=0 attention sweep + t=0 RAUQ/SinkProbe re-run)

---

### 🩻 Candidate C — "Causal evidence: Fisher direction at commit step"

> **Headline:** The Fisher top-1 direction at the commit hidden state has label-conditioned causal relevance: contradiction samples flip YES/NO under +v_top perturbation at 4× the rate of entailment samples, despite having larger logit gaps.

**Evidence:**
- Step 4: +v_top flip asymmetry (40% vs 10% at alpha=50)
- L1 larger logit gap means gap can't explain the result
- Consistent with v3 geometry: high null_ratio → dh_post ⊥ v_top → v_top is a novel direction for contradiction commits

**For:** Mechanistically novel; directly tests causality (not just correlation); the "larger gap flips more" finding is striking; connects PRI v3 to causal intervention literature.

**Against:** Pilot data only (n=20/20); confound in negative direction; alpha=50 is a large intervention; orig_answer imbalance (L1 is 75% NO vs L0 70% YES); needs logit-gap-matched sealed pre-reg. The gap between "non-null pilot" and "paper-grade evidence" is large.

**Scoring:** Cleanness ★★ · Defensibility ★★ · Cost-to-finalize ★ (needs new matched experiment)

## Recommendation

**Lead with Candidate A. Layer B as supporting evidence. Position C as forward work.**

Rationale:
1. Candidate A has the cleanest data (all 9 models, two tasks, clear 1/9 cell-transfer result) and is the most defensible (hard to argue with a replication).
2. Candidate B evidence (2/4 wins, baseline comparison) belongs in the paper but as a supporting section, not the headline — too many asterisks on the "wins."
3. Candidate C belongs in §5 (Future Work / Causal Probes), with the pilot result as motivation.

The integrated paper arc becomes:

> "We introduce **ACE (Attention Commitment Estimator)** — attention-channel calibrators at the t=0 commit step — and show it is universally discriminative across 9 models, 2 architectures, and 2 task domains, while no single cell transfers. The per-(model, task) constraint is empirically binding, not an assumption. Compared to RAUQ and SinkProbe on the same panel, ACE is competitive (2/4 clean wins, 1 loss, 1 wash), and the prior-art methods disagree in direction on the same model, suggesting they capture different phenomena. A causal pilot suggests the Fisher rupture direction is mechanistically load-bearing: contradiction samples are disproportionately sensitive to +v_top perturbation, but the pilot needs confound mitigation before promotion."
