## Question (MK)

Does reading the MoE **router** — the per-layer expert choice that sits between attention and the expert MLPs — tell us when the model's own one-token YES/NO answer is wrong, beyond the model's confidence and the attention signals?

## Design

- 🧮 **Models and data.** Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 (48 MoE layers, 128 experts, top-8) and ERNIE-4.5-21B-A3B-PT (layers 1–27 MoE, 64 experts, top-6). 2,000 HaluEval-QA rows (1,000 stems; a superset of BENCH's 500).
- 🧩 **What was captured.** Router logits at the commit position in every MoE layer, plus the full YES/NO verdict probabilities. There is no readout, because of the epsilon mismatch.
- 📏 **Analysis.** The HVS pipeline: features picked in-fold, answer-partialled, a stem-cluster bootstrap and permutations.
  - **R1:** router against full-verdict confidence.
  - **R2:** router added to attention + confidence, via in-fold logistic regression.
  - Holm across 2 models × 2 tests.
- 🔍 **Audits.** Codex wrote the code; Claude Code and DeepSeek audited it before any spend. Claude's fixes: a dtype-aware router tolerance (Qwen3's gate runs in bf16), a tie-tolerant expert check, and a re-budgeted smoke run.

## Results

- 🚧 **ERNIE-4.5-21B failed the pre-registered gate at smoke.** Validation row 1 began "The…". It was not rescued and its full run was not launched. Its instrument checks were perfect.
- ✅ **Qwen3-30B, R2 — supported.** Adding the router lifts error-detection AUROC from 0.783 to **0.811**: Δ **+0.029 [+0.016, +0.042]**, Holm p 0.002, MD-Δ 0.019. It holds under every sensitivity check: second seed +0.031, surprise baseline +0.057, strict parse.
- ➖ **Qwen3-30B, R1 — router alone against confidence.** 0.800 against 0.780, Δ +0.020 [−0.001, +0.041], Holm p 0.11. Powered but not significant, and not bounded — **tentative**.
- 🎯 **Where.** Every fold picked the same feature: **top-8 routing mass at layer 41 of 48**. Layer curve (descriptive): 0.46–0.67 in layers 0–37, 0.71–0.80 in layers 38–47.
- 📊 **Secondary checks.** The router beats attention, 0.800 against 0.750. Attention alone is worse than confidence (−0.031). Within-answer router gains are not significant.

## Answer

In the one 30B MoE that passed, watching the router adds a small, robust amount of error information beyond confidence and attention, about +0.03 AUROC. The information comes from late layers. That is evidence that routing carries signal in this model. It is **not** a general MoE claim and **not** an MoE-versus-dense comparison.
