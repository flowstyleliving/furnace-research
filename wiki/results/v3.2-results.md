### §4.5 — class-balance discovery + per-sample anomaly diagnostic (2026-05-10)

The 2026-05-10 anomaly diagnostic (`scripts/diagnostics/diagnose_v3_2_anomalies.py`) inspected sample-level decoded tokens, top-K probabilities, and Fisher r=1 at the per-model best step. It surfaced one critical methodological correction and one positive interpretability finding.

**Methodological correction — class imbalance at high gen_step**:

EOS-driven sample truncation creates *opposite-direction* class imbalance for two models at high gen_step:

| Model | Step ≥ 6 balance | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mistral 7B | 3 controls / 33 contradictions | Mistral types more after `Answer: NO` than after `Answer: YES`, so contradiction samples take longer to EOS. |
| Gemma 3 4B | 80 controls / 5 contradictions | Gemma elaborates with format-completion (`Now solve the following:...`) after `Answer: YES` but EOSes after `Answer: NO`. |

The original step sweep filtered cells by `n_total < 50` only, which let imbalanced cells (e.g., Gemma step 12 with n=85: 80/5) into the universal-winner ranking. The corrected sweep adds `min(n_control, n_contradict) >= 20` and drops these cells. Patch landed in `diagnose_v3_2_step_sweep.py`; new `n_control`, `n_contradict`, `n_min_class` columns now ship in the long-format CSV.

**Anomaly verdict updates**:
- ❌ Gemma 4B "best at step 12 (long-after-commit)" — was selection-bias artifact. Corrected balanced best: Raw r=1 @ step 3 (AUROC 0.9998).
- ❌ Mistral "best at step 13" — was selection-bias artifact (3 controls only). Corrected balanced best: Raw r=2 @ step 1 (AUROC 1.0000) or Fisher r=1 @ step 5 (1.0000).
- ✅ Llama "best at step 2 = pre-Answer" — confirmed real on balanced n=200.
- ✅ Phi-3.5 "best at step 2" — confirmed real (and step 1 is also at 1.000 on balanced data).
- ✅ Qwen 3 "best at step 2" — confirmed real.

**Positive interpretability finding — pre-Answer commitment in the top-K distribution**:

The diagnostic decoded the top-5 probabilities at each model's best step and found that for **Llama 3B, Gemma 4B, and (less directly) Qwen 3 8B**, the answer-commitment is *visible in the top-K distribution before the answer token is emitted*:

**Llama at step 2** (typing `:` after `Answer`):
```
control:        ' YES'=0.7-0.8   ' NO'=0.2-0.3   (top-1 = ':' p=1.0, but YES dominates the residual mass)
contradiction:  ' NO'=0.5-0.6   ' YES'=0.2-0.4   (NO dominates)
```

**Gemma 4B at step 3** (typing `:` after `Answer`):
```
control:        ' YES' p=1.000   (essentially deterministic)
contradiction:  ' NO'  p=1.000
```

So the rupture-at-step-2 (Llama, Phi, Qwen 3) and rupture-at-step-3 (Gemma) findings are catching a *real internal commitment* — the model has decided YES vs NO **before** it emits the answer token, and that decision shows up in the residual stream as a Fisher-detectable shift even while the next emitted token is still `:` or `Answer`.

This is a positive interpretability finding for the v3 paper line: PRI's rupture metric isn't catching token-emission noise; it's catching internal commitment that *precedes* emission. The "rupture happens in the residual stream" framing the v3 plan articulated is now empirically supported with sample-level evidence.

**Edge case — Qwen 3 conflation with COT entry**:

For Qwen 3, the rupture at step 2 is partly catching a *meta-commitment* — whether the model decides to enter chain-of-thought or to format-respond directly. Some samples produce ` Let's see...` (COT entry) instead of `:` (format entry), and that decision correlates with contradiction status (Qwen 3 enters COT more often on contradictions). So Qwen 3's step-2 rupture is conflated with "did the model decide to reason or to answer directly" — interpretability-positive but not a pure answer-commit signal.

### Mistral-Nemo and Gemma-3-1B at the commit moment

Gemma-3-1B shows the cleanest **rupture-then-drift** signature: step 1 carries virtually all discriminative signal; step 2 retains some (0.93); step 3 collapses to 0.65; steps 5+ near chance. Confirms in geometry what the raw text shows (clean YES/NO at step 1, then drift through Spanish/Marathi/Bengali/Persian — the model emits `<end_of_turn>` but generation never stops).

**Phase B universal-winner cell** (across Mistral-Nemo + Gemma-3-1B at step=1): `kl_discharged @ step 1` with min AUROC **0.9670**. The closed-form scalar from the [FALSIFIED] centered-Fisher hypothesis is the surviving load-bearing finding for cross-model-stable rupture detection.

### LOO-CV meta-classifier first pass (`scripts/diagnostics/meta_classifier_loo.py`)

Five candidate selection strategies + oracle, evaluated under leave-one-out across N=10:

| Strategy | Mean | Min | n ≥ 0.90 | n ≥ 0.95 | Mean gap |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| oracle (upper bound) | 1.000 | 0.997 | 10/10 | 10/10 | 0.000 |
| universal_kl_discharged | 0.766 | 0.519 | 3/10 | 3/10 | 0.234 |
| family_rule | 0.832 | 0.596 | 5/10 | 4/10 | 0.168 |
| nn_terminal_commit | 0.811 | 0.606 | 4/10 | 3/10 | 0.188 |
| handcrafted_v1 (2-branch) | 0.840 | 0.519 | 5/10 | 5/10 | 0.160 |
| handcrafted_v2 (6-branch) | 0.960 | 0.830 | 8/10 | 7/10 | 0.039 |
| **handcrafted_v3 (7-branch)** | **0.971** | **0.830** | **9/10** | **8/10** | **0.029** |

The handcrafted_v3 decision tree:

```
1. mean_surprise_step1 > 1.0   → Fisher r=2 @ step 3      (reasoning-tuned regime)
2. terminal_commit_rate ≥ 0.5  → Centered r=4 @ step 1    (single-token-commit)
3. family == Llama             → Raw r=2 @ step 4         (within-Llama universal)
4. family == Phi               → d_F_full @ step 2        (both Phi versions)
5. family == Gemma             → Raw r=1 @ step 3
6. family == Qwen              → Fisher r=2 @ step 3
7. mean_gen_steps < 12         → d_F_full @ step 6        (Mistral 7B v0.3 short-CoT)
8. fallback                    → kl_discharged @ step 1
```

**Key feature finding**: `mean_surprise_step1` is a single behavioral scalar that cleanly separates 3 regimes — committed-immediately (< 0.05), format-prefix/multi-step (0.1–0.9), reasoning-tuned (> 1.0). Qwen 3 sits alone above 1.0 with a 0.40 gap to the next model. The reasoning-branch is keyed on this scalar rather than a name-based tag, future-proofing it for unnamed reasoning-tuned models.

**Strict acceptance bar (min ≥ 0.90 on all N=10) is held back entirely by Qwen 3** at 0.830 (oracle Fisher r=64 @ step 2; predicted Fisher r=2 @ step 3 via the reasoning branch). With N=1 reasoning-tuned model in the training set, we can't choose between "r=2 generalizes" and "r=64 generalizes." A single additional reasoning-tuned model (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Qwen3-1.7B) would settle it.

**Phi-3.5 (0.956) and Gemma-1B (0.923)** are also below 0.95 but close — within rounding of 0.95 and a within-Gemma scale split respectively. Not bottlenecks for the strict bar.

## v4 implications

- 🚫 **Single-cell universal v4 claim is dead**. No `(gen_step, family, rank)` triple hits ≥ 0.90 across 6 models. The simplest v4 pre-reg ("PRI rupture is metric X at step Y") cannot be defended on this evidence.
- 🎯 **Per-model-optimized v4 claim is alive but suspect**. Every model has a near-perfect cell, but allowing per-model tuning at sealed-pre-reg time is post-hoc cherry-picking unless there's a held-out validation set.
- 🆕 **The honest v4 candidate** is the meta-classi[v4-candidates §4](research-candidates.md#4-meta-classifier-for-step-metric-rank-selection)ic-rank-selection)): predict the optimal cell from observable model properties so v4 becomes "given a new model, our procedure picks the right detector." **Status (2026-05-12)**: N=10 expansion complete; handcrafted_v3 tree under LOO-CV achieves mean AUROC 0.971, min 0.830, 9/10 ≥ 0.90. Strict acceptance bar held back by Qwen 3 alone (needs ≥1 more reasoning-tuned model in training set).
- 🔬 **Pre-commit step is a real phenomenon, not noise**. For Llama / Phi / Qwen 3, the rupture peaks *before* the answer is emitted. This is consistent with "the model commits internally before it commits in tokens" — a real interpretability finding worth its own writeup.
- 🚫 **Gemma 4B's step-12 anomaly was an artifact** (class imbalance: 80 controls / 5 contradictions because Gemma's contradictions EOS at step 4). Retracted; corrected best is step 3, consistent with the other models. The §4.5 diagnostic surfaced this; the patched sweep script now filters by `min_n_per_class ≥ 20`.
- 📐 **Adaptive vs fixed**: the "find the right moment" intuition was right but the implementation was wrong. v4 needs *step search*, not commit-step pinning. A fixed-step pre-reg per model (selected on a held-out validation set) is more defensible than per-sample adaptive measurement.
- 💡 **Pre-Answer commitment is a real interpretability finding.** §4.5 evidence shows the model's top-K probability distribution encodes YES/NO commitment 1-3 steps *before* the answer token is emitted (Llama at step 2 typing `:`, Gemma at step 3 typing `:`). The Fisher-pullback rupture metric catches this internal commitment in the residual stream, supporting the v3 paper's "rupture in residual stream precedes token emission" framing.
